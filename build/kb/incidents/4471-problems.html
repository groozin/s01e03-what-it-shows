Counted the desk's way: a report counts under the cause it is filed under, and a one-day move counts there too. Each problem is one ticket; the reports column is how many reports that ticket covers.

  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month come out swapped, on every channel.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants on booking-v3 type the month first, as told, and the form reads it day first. Only through the form, only on booking-v3. A parser fix does not reach it.
  • one-day: the booking moves by one day, or arrives a day early. A month-first parser swaps day and month; it never moves a date by one day. The desk read these right (desk-notes.json), then filed them under parser by tenant. Look at time zones and DST.

How these were counted

  • One report per id. The ticket export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (its locale and its booking form, locales.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and disagrees on dozens of reports.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move is its own problem. As in the desk’s own table, it still counts under the cause it is filed under, so the counts add up to more than the reports.
